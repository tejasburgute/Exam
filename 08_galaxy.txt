


###########  WES – Download FASTQ data

# Create folder
mkdir WES_Subset
cd WES_Subset

# Download SRA data
prefetch SRR3315784

# Convert SRA into paired FASTQ
fasterq-dump SRR3315784 --split-files -e 4

# Compress FASTQ files
gzip SRR3315784_1.fastq
gzip SRR3315784_2.fastq

##############---WES – Prepare 100,000 read pairs-----------------#################
# R1 subset
seqtk sample -s100 SRR3315784_1.fastq.gz 100000 | gzip > SRR3315784_1_subset.fastq.gz

# R2 subset (same seed)
seqtk sample -s100 SRR3315784_2.fastq.gz 100000 | gzip > SRR3315784_2_subset.fastq.gz

####################-----RNA-Seq – Download data------------######################
# Download sample
prefetch SRR_ACCESSION

# Convert to FASTQ
fasterq-dump SRR_ACCESSION --split-files -e 4

# Compress
gzip SRR_ACCESSION_1.fastq
gzip SRR_ACCESSION_2.fastq


##################---------RNA-Seq – Prepare subsets-------------#######################
# Control 1
seqtk sample -s101 Control_1_R1.fastq.gz 100000 | gzip > Control_1_R1_subset.fastq.gz
seqtk sample -s101 Control_1_R2.fastq.gz 100000 | gzip > Control_1_R2_subset.fastq.gz

# Control 2
seqtk sample -s102 Control_2_R1.fastq.gz 100000 | gzip > Control_2_R1_subset.fastq.gz
seqtk sample -s102 Control_2_R2.fastq.gz 100000 | gzip > Control_2_R2_subset.fastq.gz

# Treatment 1
seqtk sample -s103 Treatment_1_R1.fastq.gz 100000 | gzip > Treatment_1_R1_subset.fastq.gz
seqtk sample -s103 Treatment_1_R2.fastq.gz 100000 | gzip > Treatment_1_R2_subset.fastq.gz

# Treatment 2
seqtk sample -s104 Treatment_2_R1.fastq.gz 100000 | gzip > Treatment_2_R1_subset.fastq.gz
seqtk sample -s104 Treatment_2_R2.fastq.gz 100000 | gzip > Treatment_2_R2_subset.fastq.gz



#######################____----Verify subsets------------############################
# Check WES
zcat SRR3315784_1_subset.fastq.gz | wc -l
zcat SRR3315784_2_subset.fastq.gz | wc -l

# Check RNA-seq
zcat Control_1_R1_subset.fastq.gz | wc -l
zcat Control_1_R2_subset.fastq.gz | wc -l

# Check number of reads
zcat Control_1_R1_subset.fastq.gz | awk 'END{print NR/4}'

# Display first two reads
zcat Control_1_R1_subset.fastq.gz | head -8
zcat Control_1_R2_subset.fastq.gz | head -8







WES ANALYSIS – GALAXY

1. Upload FASTQ (R1, R2)
2. FastQC – Quality check
3. MultiQC – Combine reports
4. Trimmomatic – Read trimming
5. FastQC – Check trimmed reads
6. BWA-MEM – Read alignment
7. SAMtools Sort – Sort BAM
8. SAMtools Index – Index BAM
9. Picard MarkDuplicates – Mark duplicates
10. SAMtools flagstat – Alignment statistics
11. FreeBayes – Variant calling
12. BCFtools norm – Normalize variants
13. BCFtools filter – Filter variants
14. SnpEff – Variant annotation
15. ClinVar / gnomAD – Variant interpretation
16. IGV – Variant visualization

FASTQ → QC → TRIM → ALIGN → BAM
→ VARIANT CALLING → FILTER → ANNOTATE
→ INTERPRET







RNA seq

**RNA-SEQ ANALYSIS – GALAXY**

1. Upload FASTQ – Upload paired-end reads
2. FastQC – Quality check
3. MultiQC – Combine quality reports
4. Cutadapt – Adapter and quality trimming
5. FastQC – Check trimmed reads
6. HISAT2 – Read alignment to dm6 reference
7. SAMtools Sort – Sort BAM files
8. FeatureCounts – Count reads per gene using GTF
9. DESeq2 – Differential gene expression analysis
10. Volcano Plot – Visualize differentially expressed genes

**FASTQ → QC → TRIM → ALIGN → SORT → COUNT**\
**→ DESeq2 → DIFFERENTIAL EXPRESSION → VOLCANO**
